🛒 Where is the revenue really coming from, and where is the risk?

I analysed 10,000 Amazon India orders (2024-2026) to find out.

Imagine you are a business leader who needs answers to four questions:
❓ Which categories and states drive sales and profit?
❓ How do customers pay, and how are orders fulfilled?
❓ How healthy is order quality?
❓ Where are the hidden risks?

To answer them, I built a 5-page interactive Power BI dashboard on a publicly available dataset of 10,000 rows. Here is what the data said 👇

📌 THE BIG PICTURE
💰 Sales of ₹155.79M → profit of ₹33.17M (21.29% margin)
📦 24,926 units, with an average order value of ₹15.58K

1️⃣ Revenue is concentrated in one category
🔥 Electronics & Mobiles = ₹123.57M, about 79% of sales, even though every category holds a similar 21-23% of the product range.
👉 Risk: a slowdown in Electronics would affect the whole business.

2️⃣ A few states dominate
📍 Maharashtra (₹27M), Karnataka (₹22M) and Delhi (₹20M) = about 44% of sales.
👉 Opportunity: the remaining states are largely untapped.

3️⃣ Customers prefer digital payments
💳 UPI = 50.4%, cards = 19.94%, Cash on Delivery = only 14.83%.

4️⃣ Fulfilment relies on Amazon
🚚 FBA = 69%, Seller Flex = 20%, Merchant (FBM) = 11%.

5️⃣ Order quality is healthy, with category hotspots
✅ 8,147 orders (81.47%) delivered, return rate 4.85%, average discount 8.56%.
⚠️ Apparel & Fashion has the most cancellations (148) and Electronics the most returns (164).

🎯 MY RECOMMENDATIONS (based on this dataset only)
• Grow Home & Kitchen (#2 category, ₹17.97M) to reduce dependence on Electronics
• Focus on states ranked 11 and below
• Investigate the causes of Apparel cancellations and Electronics returns

📑 Pages: Executive Overview → Category & Product Performance → Order Analysis → Payment, Fulfillment & Geographic Analytics → Executive Summary
